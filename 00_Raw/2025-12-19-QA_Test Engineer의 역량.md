## PM과 QA 
Generally, pm and qa manages different part of functions.

** PM(Product Manager)**: Stand objects of project, leading team, and manages to meet  schedule, asset, and quality statndard.
 
 **QA(Qualify Assurance)**:  verify if product's requirements and quality standard meets, and responsible for overall Development process.
 
 These two independent role has different responsibility, but have to corporate deep to success to the product goal.
 
 ## 2. Responsibiilities of PM
 PM is charge of planning, managinig resources and team. 
 The key role is:
 
** managing Schedule and standardlizing KPIS.**
 Define main objectives of the project, and establish detail schedule and milestones.
 
** Distribute resources and Priortiize decision making.**
 Distribute time and resources within limited time and resources.
 
** Manages team communication**

 ## 3. Responsibiilities of QA
 Ensure whether the product is developed right based on requirements and quality standards. 
 
 **Plan Test Plan**
 Plan test strategy and detail plans in initial product making so that the product can meet the expected quality standard. 
 
 **Execute the test and manage issues**
 Identify Errors and issues in advance, record and share to teams
 
 **Improve process**
 Identify the searched problems, analyze it and contribute to evaluate efficiency while development and CI process
